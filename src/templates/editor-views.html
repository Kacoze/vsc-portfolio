# Kamil Kozieradzki
> QA & Test Automation Lead at BF Games
> 8 yrs · Playwright · TypeScript · CI/CD · Available for consulting
I've spent 8 years making sure other people's code doesn't break in production. Still enjoying it.
## Background
Currently I lead QA and test automation for sportsbook and casino platforms
at BF Games, working across multiple brands and large frontend codebases.
My work includes test architecture, automation frameworks, CI integration,
and improving development workflows so testing scales with the product.
Background in front-end development (JavaScript / TypeScript) helps me
approach testing from a developer perspective. I focus on pragmatic
automation, tooling, and processes that reduce manual effort while
improving release confidence.
## Approach
- **Testing as part of the process** — I embed QA from the start of
development, not as a gate at the end. Bugs caught early cost less.
- **Frameworks that survive scale** — every project I join gets a
maintainable automation layer: typed, reviewed, and running in CI.
- **People, not just pipelines** — I lead a team of 8.
The tooling matters, but so does making sure everyone
actually uses it and understands why.
